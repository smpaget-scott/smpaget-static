A little about me

About Scott

I have worked with young people since 1996, in church, voluntary and statutory settings. My work has included supporting youth workers, developing practice and exploring the role of faith and community in young people's lives.

My academic interests include youth work, theology, ethics, digital culture and research. My master's research explored youth workers' use of computer games and questions of ethics and play. More recent interests include church youth work, parish geography and podcasting. I am a member of IASYM, AMYE, AAR and SST.

I have a Doctorate in Practical Theology from the University of Glasgow, studying work with young people within the Church of Scotland alongside a Master's in Theology from the University of Glasgow, PGC in Community Education from the University of Strathclyde, and a BA in Youth Work with Applied Theology from International Christian College. I have a background as a welder and hold ECITB certification.
